Checked 2026-11-15.

Result: no true case-officer guidance was found or could be obtained. The 4 files
here are internal orders (zarządzenia) of the Commander-in-Chief of the Border Guard
from his official journal, i.e. normative acts, kept only because they are the

This result is weaker than it should be: the two hosts that would carry UdSC and

Who decides
- Voivodes (wojewodowie), foreigners' departments of the voivodeship offices: temporary
  and permanent residence, long-term EU residence, single permits, EU Blue Cards, work
  permits (zezwolenia na pracę).
- Head of the Office for Foreigners (Szef Urzędu do Spraw Cudzoziemców, UdSC): appeals
  against voivodes' decisions; international protection at first instance; Dublin;
  supervises and trains voivodes' staff (Act on Foreigners).
- Refugee Board (Rada do Spraw Uchodźców): asylum appeals.
- Border Guard (Straż Graniczna): refusal of entry, return decisions, detention in
  guarded centres, escorts, SIS alerts.
- District labour offices (starostowie): declarations on entrusting work; the labour
  ministry (MRPiPS) issues explanations of the 20 March 2025 Act on the conditions for
  entrusting work to foreigners (in force 1 June 2025).

EU Blue Card / temporary work agencies
- No document obtained addresses the EU Blue Card, temporary work agencies or employers
  of record. These matters are governed by the Act on Foreigners and the 2025 Act on
  entrusting work to foreigners (statutes, not collected). The labour ministry answered
  a Sejm interpellation (i10516) that the 2025 act does not restrict process
  outsourcing and that voivodes have difficulty establishing the real subject of such
  service contracts - evidence that the question is live, but no published guidance on
  it was found.

How much real guidance: none found. Poland's deciding officials work from the statutes,
ministerial regulations and unpublished letters and instructions of the Head of UdSC
and of the labour ministry; the only published internal rules are a handful of Border
Guard orders on escorts, detention and SIS entries.
