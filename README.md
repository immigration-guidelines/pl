# Immigration guidance: Poland

Published guidance that the authorities of Poland issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 8 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Little is published for Polish decision-makers, and the Office for Foreigners' own sites could not be checked: its public information bulletin answers with a bot check, and its main sites did not answer. This repository holds four orders of the Border Guard Commander-in-Chief (2016 to 2024) on escorting foreigners, guarded centres and Schengen Information System entries, marked `procedural act`. The Office for Foreigners' guidelines to the voivodes are known to exist but are not published.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
